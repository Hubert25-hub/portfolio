CASE STUDY

ACTUEL

News website for a Togolese media outlet.

The brief

ACTUEL

ACTUEL needed a complete rebuild of its news website: a fast, readable platform to publish articles across 10 categories, with search and newsletter signup.

What was done

Rebuilt as a fast static site: homepage with a breaking-news ticker, category and article pages rendered from a data file, on-site search, related articles, reader location and weather display, editorial charter and legal pages, SEO metadata and sitemap.

The result

A live, mobile-friendly news site, currently in its final content phase before launch.
