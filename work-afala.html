CASE STUDY

AFALA Immigration

Website for a Canadian immigration firm, with an eligibility test and a client file tracker.

The brief

AFALA Immigration

AFALA Immigration Inc, a Canadian immigration firm, needed a website that builds trust before anything else: present the firm and its consultant, let visitors test their eligibility, and turn interest into WhatsApp conversations and booked consultations.

What was done

Designed a six page website in a white and red Canadian identity: the consultant presented first, a slideshow of Canadian landscapes, an eligibility test that sends its result to WhatsApp, contact and booking forms, and a complete graphic charter. A private client file tracker was also built for the firm, with follow up reminders and a PDF file export.

The result

The website is built and in its final content phase with the firm, ahead of launch.
