CASE STUDY

ayePREP

Brand identity for a Canadian exam-prep platform.

The brief

ayePREP

ayePREP, an AYEGROUPE sub-brand (ayeprep.com), needed a full identity for its French-language TCF and TEF Canada exam-prep platform. A logo that signals Canada and education, built to the AYEGROUPE guidelines.

What was done

Designed a logo combining a red and navy maple leaf inside a navy circle with an open book at its base, plus the ayePREP wordmark in Archivo. Three iterations, then the app icon, iPhone mockups, and a 10-page brand guideline document.

The result

A client-approved logo, live on the platform, delivered with a full export package: PNG, JPG, favicons, guidelines and README.
