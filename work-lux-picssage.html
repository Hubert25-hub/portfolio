CASE STUDY

Lux Picssage Massage

Website for a massage therapy clinic in St. Albert, Canada.

The brief

Lux Picssage Massage

Lux Picssage Massage LTD needed a complete online presence: a website to present the clinic, its services and prices, and to let clients book appointments easily.

What was done

Designed and built a responsive one-page website. Online booking integrated through Jane App, services and pricing sections, photo gallery, testimonials area and a 5-question FAQ.

The result

A fast, mobile-friendly website, live and used by the clinic to receive bookings.
