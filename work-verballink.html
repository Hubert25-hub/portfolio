CASE STUDY

Verbal Link Institute

Complete bilingual website for a French language institute: courses, online placement test and shop.

The brief

Verbal Link Institute

Verbal Link Institute, a French language training institute in Burnaby, Canada, needed to leave its monthly website builder for a fast site it fully owns: bilingual pages for its TCF and TEF Canada courses, government exam preparation, a free placement test and an online shop.

What was done

Rebuilt the complete site as 18 bilingual pages: course pages for every level, a real online placement test with 32 scored questions, a writing task and an instant level result, a working registration form, and a shop presenting its 9 courses and books. The design follows the institute's own logo, in navy and green, with the logo carried across every page.

The result

A complete, mobile friendly website, ready to move to the institute's own domain, with no monthly builder fee: only yearly hosting.
